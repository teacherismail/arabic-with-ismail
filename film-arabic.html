Dialects Offered

  • Egyptian Arabic: The absolute standard for media, comedy, and drama, understood by virtually 100% of the Arab world.
  • Levantine Arabic: Native to Jordan, Palestine, Syria, and Lebanon — crucial for realistic contemporary narratives.
  • Modern Standard Arabic (Fus'ha): Essential for historical epics, military protocols, formal speeches, or broadcast journalism scenes.
Why Productions Lose Immersion

Why Hollywood Often Gets Arabic Wrong

We have all seen major studio projects break immersion for millions of viewers. A character written as a native of Cairo suddenly speaking with a heavy Levantine accent, or a tactical scene using mismatched Gulf military vocabulary. These structural inconsistencies pull native audiences completely out of the narrative. My role is to bulletproof your project against these accurate cultural critiques.
