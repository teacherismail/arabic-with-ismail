Questions

Common Questions

Is the free session really free?
Completely. No credit card, no account, no strings. Book a time, show up, and meet Ismail. That's it. The free session is 45 minutes of real Arabic learning — not a sales pitch.
Do Prime sessions expire?
No. Your Prime sessions have no expiry date. The calendar is always open 31 days ahead — book whenever you're ready, one session at a time, at your own pace.
What's the difference between Regular and Hollywood/Diplomat?
Regular sessions cover any Arabic goal — dialect, culture, literature, conversation. Hollywood & Diplomat sessions are specialist coaching for specific high-stakes needs: preparing for a film role, a scene, a negotiation, or diplomatic protocol.
Can I get a refund on Prime sessions?
Yes — on unused sessions. If you've purchased Prime 11 and used 4 sessions, we'll refund the remaining 7 sessions. Completed sessions are non-refundable. Single sessions are non-refundable once booked.
How do I pay?
All payments are processed securely online via Stripe or PayPal. Full payment is required upfront. You'll receive a confirmation and your booking link immediately after payment.
Can I mix session types in one Prime package?
Prime packages cover Regular sessions ($45 each). Hollywood & Diplomat sessions are booked separately at $79 each. If you need a mix, contact Ismail directly to discuss a custom arrangement.
What if I need to reschedule?
No problem at all. Reschedule directly through the booking link before your session time. No penalty, no questions asked. Life happens — we get it.
Why prime numbers for the packages?
Because Arabic learning is anything but ordinary. Prime numbers are indivisible — just like the commitment to truly learning a language. It's also a nod to the mathematical elegance found throughout Arabic and Islamic civilization.
